# Engagement Templates

This skill provides standard templates for every document type in a My Genie client engagement. Use these as starting points. Customize with client-specific details, but keep the structure consistent.

## When to Use Which Template

| Situation | Template |
|-----------|----------|
| Client agrees to proceed | Statement of Work (SOW) |
| Tracking deliverables | Deliverable Checklist (per pillar) |
| Post-onboarding | Welcome Email |
| After first meeting | Kickoff Recap Email |
| During active engagement | Weekly Update Email |
| Completing a milestone | Deliverable Handoff Email |
| Sending an invoice | Invoice Email |
| Engagement complete | Wrap-Up Email |
| Reaching out to past client | Re-Engagement Email |

## Document Principles

1. **Use brand-voice for all copy.** Every template follows the tone rules in the brand-voice skill.
2. **Keep it short.** Clients don't read long documents. If a section runs past one page, cut it.
3. **Lead with what matters to the client.** Deliverables and timeline first. Terms and conditions last.
4. **Real numbers only.** No placeholder "TBD" in client-facing documents. If you don't know, find out first.
5. **One call to action per email.** Don't ask the client to do five things. Ask them to do one thing.

## Email Templates

### Welcome Email (Post-Onboarding)

**Subject:** Welcome to My Genie -- here's what happens next

Body:

Hey [First Name],

Welcome aboard. Here's what's coming:

1. Kickoff call -- [Date/Time]. We'll walk through your tools and map your current setup.
2. Access -- I'll need admin access to [tools identified during discovery]. I'll send a separate email with specifics.
3. Timeline -- Your [Audit/Optimization/Build] starts the day after kickoff. You'll see results within the first week.

Questions before we start? Just reply here.

-- Bobby

### Kickoff Recap Email

**Subject:** Kickoff recap + next steps

Body:

Hey [First Name],

Good call today. Here's what we covered:

Your current setup: [2-3 sentence summary of tools and workflows discussed]

What we're doing first: [Specific first action]

What I need from you: [Specific ask -- e.g., "Admin access to HubSpot and Mailchimp by EOD Friday"]

Next check-in: [Date/Time]

-- Bobby

### Weekly Update Email

**Subject:** Weekly update -- [Client Name] -- [Date]

Body:

Hey [First Name],

Quick update on this week:

Done:
- [Completed item 1]
- [Completed item 2]

In progress:
- [Current item -- expected completion date]

Next week:
- [Upcoming item 1]
- [Upcoming item 2]

Anything you need from me? Let me know before our check-in on [Day].

-- Bobby

### Deliverable Handoff Email

**Subject:** [Deliverable name] is ready

Body:

Hey [First Name],

[Deliverable name] is done. Here's what's in it:

- [Key point 1]
- [Key point 2]
- [Key point 3]

Full report: [Notion link]

Take a look when you get a chance. I'd like to walk through it on our next call ([Day at Time]).

-- Bobby

### Invoice Email

**Subject:** Invoice -- [Description] -- $[Amount]

Body:

Hey [First Name],

Invoice attached for [description].

Amount: $[Amount]
Due: [Date]
Pay online: [Stripe payment link]

Questions about the invoice? Just reply here.

-- Bobby

### Wrap-Up Email

**Subject:** Engagement complete -- here's what we built

Body:

Hey [First Name],

We're wrapped up. Here's the summary:

What we did:
- [Deliverable 1 + result]
- [Deliverable 2 + result]
- [Deliverable 3 + result]

By the numbers:
- [Key metric 1 -- e.g., "CRM utilization: 30% to 87%"]
- [Key metric 2 -- e.g., "Lead response time: 4 hours to 47 seconds"]
- [Key metric 3 -- e.g., "Monthly tool waste eliminated: $340"]

What's next: To keep things running and improving, the retainer ($500/month) covers 20 new skills per month, new tool connections, and model upgrades as they drop. Want me to set it up?

One more thing: If you know someone drowning in tools they don't use, send them my way. I'll run a free 15-minute audit call. No pitch, just numbers. You get $500 off your next invoice for every referral that becomes a client.

It's been good working with you.

-- Bobby

### Re-Engagement Email

**Subject:** Quick question about [their business/industry]

Body:

Hey [First Name],

It's been a few months since we wrapped up. Wanted to check in:

1. How's [specific thing we set up] holding up?
2. Anything broken or slowing down?

Also -- [relevant industry update or new capability].

Worth a 15-minute catch-up? [Calendly link]

-- Bobby
