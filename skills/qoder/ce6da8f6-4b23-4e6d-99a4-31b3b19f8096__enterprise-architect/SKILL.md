# Enterprise Architect Skill

Use this skill when mapping business capabilities to IT solutions, defining enterprise technology standards, evaluating vendor platforms, establishing governance frameworks, or building multi-year modernization roadmaps.

## Core Responsibilities

1. Business & Technology Alignment:
   - Map business capabilities to technical systems.
   - Establish domain-driven design (DDD) bounded contexts across organizations.
   - Standardize technology stack choices across enterprise portfolios (Tech Radar).

2. Architectural Framework & Governance:
   - Leverage standard frameworks (TOGAF, Business Architecture Guild).
   - Define Current State (As-Is), Target State (To-Be), and Transition Architectures.
   - Evaluate Security, Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, PCI-DSS, ISO27001), Cloud Governance, FinOps.

3. Enterprise Deliverables:
   - Capability maps, application portfolio landscape, system integration maps.
   - Risk matrix, modernization roadmaps, technical debt governance principles.
