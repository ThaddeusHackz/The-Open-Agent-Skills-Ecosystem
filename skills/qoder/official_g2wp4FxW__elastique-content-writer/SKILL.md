## Content Frameworks by Type

### 📝 Blog Posts / Long-Form Educational

**Structure:**
```
Hook (1-2 sentences) — provocative question or surprising fact about the body
↓
Problem (2-3 paragraphs) — what's actually happening physiologically
↓
Science (2-3 paragraphs) — explain the mechanism (lymphatic, circulatory)
↓
Solution (1-2 paragraphs) — how Elastique's technology addresses this
↓
Evidence (1 paragraph) — clinical data, testimonials, expert quotes
↓
CTA (1 sentence) — restrained, access-framed
```

**Word count:** 800–1,500 words
**Tone:** Educational mode — clinical-adjacent but never cold
**SEO:** Include target keyword in H1 and first 100 words. Use related terms naturally.

**Example hooks:**
- "Your lymphatic system doesn't have a pump. That's not a design flaw — it's a clue."
- "By the time you feel ankle swelling on a flight, your circulation has been compromised for hours."
- "The difference between aging well and aging fast may come down to a system most people have never heard of."

### 📧 Email Copy

**Types and structures:**

#### Welcome Sequence (3-5 emails)
| Email | Purpose | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1: Welcome | Thank + brand story + what to expect | Warm, personal (Emeline voice) |
| 2: Education | How MicroPerle technology works | Educational, specific |
| 3: Social Proof | Customer results + testimonials | Evidence-based, understated |
| 4: Segment Angle | Content tailored to their entry point | Relevant to their segment |
| 5: First Offer | Restrained promotional — access framing | "Now is a good time to start" |

#### Promotional Email
```
Subject line: Benefit-led, curiosity-driven (never urgency-based)
Preheader: Extends the subject line thought
Hero line: One sentence that creates desire before naming the offer
Body: 2-3 sentences max — specific benefit + what's being offered
CTA: Single button, action-oriented
```

**Subject line rules:**
- ✅ Benefit-first: "What happens to your circulation after 3 hours of sitting"
- ✅ Curiosity: "The system your body is missing"
- ✅ Emeline's voice: "I almost didn't tell you about this"
- ❌ Urgency: "LAST CHANCE! 24 hours left!!!"
- ❌ Clickbait: "You won't believe what this does"
- ❌ Generic: "New arrivals are here"

Always provide **3 subject line options** with each email.

### 📱 Social Media Captions

```

↓
2-3 sentences expanding the point (education or social proof)

```

```

↓

Key insight or data point
↓

```
Tone: Insightful, expert, slightly more formal. No hashtag spam.

### 🏷️ Product Descriptions

**Structure:**
```
Headline: Benefit-first (not product name first)
Subhead: The "science you can wear" hook
↓
Paragraph 1: What the product does for YOUR body (feature → benefit)
Paragraph 2: How MicroPerle technology makes it work (mechanism)
Paragraph 3: When/where to wear it (use cases)
↓
Bullet points: 4-6 specific features with benefit annotation
↓
Proof point: Clinical data or testimonial snippet
```

**Feature-to-benefit formula:**
Always translate features into outcomes:
- MicroPerle beads → lymphatic stimulation → reduced swelling, smoother skin
- Gradient compression → enhanced blood flow → faster recovery, more energy
- Luxury fabric → all-day comfort → actual compliance → actual results

### 🎓 Educational Content (Science Explainers)

**Structure:**
```
Myth or misconception (hook)
↓
What's actually happening (anatomy/physiology, simplified but accurate)
↓
Why it matters for [audience segment's goal]
↓
What you can do about it (actionable, Elastique positioned as one solution among lifestyle changes)
↓
Deeper reading / expert reference
```

**Rules for science content:**
- Name specific anatomical systems (lymphatic, venous, arterial)
- Use precise mechanisms (MicroPerle bead mechanics, pressure-point stimulation)
- Never say "detox" or "cleanse" — say "lymphatic drainage" or "circulation enhancement"
- Cite specific outcomes, not vague claims
- It's okay to discuss the science broadly before connecting to Elastique — we educate first, sell second
